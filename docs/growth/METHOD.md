# The method the app serves

**Big bets on new hours, marginal gains in every hour.**

- **Big bets:** five or six large moves that open unsold time, such as a late-night bar, weekend brunch, hire and events. Owned by leadership, reviewed quarterly, each passing three gates: feasible, pilot made money, still makes money at scale.
- **Marginal gains:** small tested improvements to service, product, pace, cost and guest return. Owned by the whole team, three or four live at a time.

## Six rules

1. If we can't measure it, we don't claim it. Every gain names the one number it moves.
2. One change at a time.
3. The guest comes first. Every test has a guest guardrail.
4. Anyone can propose, everyone hears the result.
5. No blame for a failed test.
6. Bank it. A gain counts once it is written into how we work and still holds after 90 days.

## The weekly loop

Spot → Size → Test → Share → Standardise.

## Gain areas

Welcome and bookings; Floor service; Bar; Kitchen; Events and hire; Guests and reputation; Cost and margin; Team. Each has a captain.

## What this means for the app

- An **idea** is four lines: what would change, which number it should move, how to test it, what could go wrong for the guest.
- A **test** has one primary metric, one guardrail, a design, dates, a pass mark and an owner.
- A **gain** is a test that passed, with its measured change and yearly value, re-checked at 90 days.
- The **gains board** shows ideas, live tests and banked gains, with a running total.
- The **weekly scorecard** is what the weekly trading review reads.
- Limits to enforce or warn on: more than four live tests in a venue; two live tests on the same primary metric.

## Example test card

- **Name:** First drink in two minutes
- **Change:** within two minutes of seating, the server offers a named drink
- **Primary metric:** drinks per cover (`dpc`)
- **Guardrail:** turn time (`turn`) unchanged
- **Design:** half the sections, swapping weekly, three weeks
- **Pass mark:** +5%
